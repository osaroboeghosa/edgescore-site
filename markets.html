Gauge: +1 or -1 per asset from its position vs the 50-day average, summed (range -6 to +6); gold, VIX and DXY count inverted. ATR ratio = 14-day ATR vs its 100-day average (under 0.8 compressed, over 1.2 expanded). Rate spread = base currency rate minus quote currency rate, in percentage points; positive favours holding the base. Rates mix policy and 3-month interbank series and can be months old, so they are approximate. Carry matrix = your engine's carry score (-1 to +1). Not financial advice.
